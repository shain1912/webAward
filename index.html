About

Twelve years, three countries

Good product design is quiet. It removes friction until the interface disappears and only the intent remains.

I'm Mara — a principal product designer based in Copenhagen, previously in Berlin and Seoul. For twelve years I've worked where ambiguity is highest: zero-to-one products, gnarly enterprise flows, and design systems that have to hold up under scale.

My practice pairs sharp systems thinking with an obsessive eye for typography and motion. I prototype in code, test with real users, and stay through delivery — design isn't done until it ships.
